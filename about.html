Our Story

A New Standard For Technology Delivery

DTC Technology Solutions was established in 2025 as part of El Eyad Group, one of the largest construction groups in the Egypt. That heritage gives DTC something most technology integrators don't have: the discipline, scale and project-delivery muscle of a top-tier construction group, applied to IT and low-current systems.

In a short time, DTC has become a partner of choice for organizations seeking to adopt cutting-edge solutions — backed by a multidisciplinary team of presales consultants, project managers and technical engineers.

Our teams bring extensive, hands-on expertise across public sector, healthcare, banking and commercial industries — translating complex requirements into infrastructure that simply works, on schedule and on budget.
